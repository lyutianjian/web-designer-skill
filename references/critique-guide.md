# Visual Design Critique

Use for a requested design review. Review the artifact against its purpose and constraints; a critique does not automatically authorize redesign or full browser acceptance.

## Ground the review

Use the supplied render, live UI, or other available evidence. Distinguish visible findings from behavior that was not exercised. With code alone, describe likely issues as hypotheses rather than claiming to have seen the rendered result.

| Lens | Look for |
|---|---|
| Fit | Whether the design supports the audience, task, and intended identity. |
| Hierarchy | Reading order, emphasis, grouping, legibility, and useful density. |
| Craft | Alignment, spacing relationships, typography, image treatment, and consistent tokens. |
| Usability | Clear actions, relevant states, keyboard access, focus, and responsive behavior. |
| Character | A coherent visual voice appropriate to the brief; novelty is valuable when it improves the result. |

Weight these lenses by the artifact. A dashboard values scanning and comparison; slides value legibility at a distance; a campaign may rely more on imagery or motion. Fixed font counts, type ratios, or whitespace percentages cannot establish quality on their own.

Use the relevant section of [failure patterns](failure-patterns.md) only when it helps diagnose an observed issue. Supplied brand and product requirements take precedence over generic aesthetic preferences.

## Make findings actionable

Lead with consequential findings. For each, identify the location or state, the observed problem, its effect on the user, and a concrete improvement. Separate defects from optional aesthetic alternatives; retain strengths that a repair could accidentally remove.

Use scores or a formal rubric when requested or useful for comparing versions. Explain the basis and evidence, rather than imposing a fixed set of numeric thresholds. Choose the number of findings and level of detail to match the work.

Report findings for review-only requests. When implementation or repairs are authorized, make the appropriate changes and verify their effects. Use [browser acceptance](browser-acceptance.md) when requested or when an interaction or responsive-layout question requires that evidence.
