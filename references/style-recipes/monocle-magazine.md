# monocle-magazine — Monocle-Inspired Editorial

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Editorial / Minimalist. Journals, travel, hospitality, and stories grounded in people and places.
**Reference points**: Monocle publications, photography, and editorial layouts.

## Starting palette and type

- Cream `#F2EFE7`; ink `#1A1A1A`; red `#C7322E`; olive `#5E6347`; rule `#C8C2B0`.
- Serif options: Plantin, Mercury, or Tiempos for headlines and reading text; a restrained sans such as Söhne can distinguish labels.
- Body text around 16–17px and line height around 1.6 provide a starting point. Keep kickers and captions legible rather than making them tiny for effect.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96; wider layouts can use measured columns and fine rules.
- Crisp edges, restrained elevation, and quiet transitions support the printed-page association.
- Use a kicker, headline, and standfirst when each adds information. Italics and pull-quotes can introduce a second reading pace.
- Place-rich photography, purposeful crops, and captions can connect the page to a real setting.
- Adapt multi-column layouts to a clear narrow-screen reading order.

## Tradeoffs

Editorial craft can benefit a product page when there is useful narrative to tell. It need not prohibit sans-serif headings, cards, or additional semantic colors. Retain scanning aids when users need to compare or act quickly, and use authentic source imagery for documentary claims.
