# stripe-press — Stripe Press-Inspired Books and Ideas

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Warm Humanist / Editorial. Publications, thoughtful product stories, and content where the physical object matters.
**Reference points**: Stripe Press books, material details, and editorial product photography.

## Starting palette and type

- Bone `#F1ECDE`; deeper surface `#E6DCC4`; ink `#1A1A18`; secondary text `#736D5A`; rule `#C8BEA4`.
- Teal `#1B4B5A` or sienna `#A04A2A` can echo a cover, binding, or illustration.
- Display options: GT Sectra, Domaine Display, or Italian Old Style. Source Serif, Lyon Text, or ITC Galliard can support extended reading.
- A clear sans can support navigation and labels. Body text around 18–19px and line height around 1.65 are useful examples.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96, with pauses where the narrative changes pace.
- Product photography can show a book's binding, paper, and physical depth. Warm directional light and a grounded shadow can emphasize material.
- Italics, a pull-quote, or a wide margin can distinguish a second reading layer.
- Texture and cover-specific color can connect sections while keeping body text on a quiet surface.
- Gentle transitions or a small object rotation can support material exploration when interaction adds value.

## Tradeoffs

Warm editorial cues can extend beyond books when the content supports them. Catalog grids, sans-serif text, white backgrounds, and multiple cover colors may be appropriate. Keep browsing, comparison, and actions clear rather than requiring every item to occupy a full feature section.

## Optional image study

> Cloth-bound book on bone #F1ECDE paper, warm raking light, visible spine texture and grounded soft shadow, teal or sienna detail, 4:5. Use supplied cover assets when representing an actual publication.
