# headspace-meditation — Headspace-Inspired Care and Calm

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Warm Humanist. Wellness, learning, and services that benefit from reassuring, approachable communication.
**Reference points**: Headspace illustration and product material; use the supplied brand when a different wellness product is intended.

## Starting palette and type

- Peach `#FFE2C5` or `#FFEDD5`; coral `#F4A573`; ink `#1B3A47`; secondary text `#5C6B7A`.
- Supporting accents: lavender `#B0A5D1`, sage `#9DB67A`, and salmon `#F5867B`.
- A friendly sans such as Apercu or a suitable project family can support the tone. Use expressive headings without sacrificing clear reading text.
- Body text around 16–17px is a starting point; an occasional handwritten accent may fit a particular message.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96; radius examples 16 / 24 / 32px where soft grouping is helpful.
- Rounded illustration and warm surfaces can suggest care. Distinguish clickable controls from decorative shapes.
- Gentle transitions can support feedback and pacing. For an actual guided exercise, use the timing and content of the provided program.
- Calm, specific microcopy can explain what happens next without making unsupported health claims.
- A reduced-motion presentation should retain both instruction and task completion.

## Tradeoffs

Cooler colors, crisp elements, more contrast, and expert language can all fit a caring product. Avoid making every surface equally soft when users need clear structure. Use actual brand assets for recognizable mascots; an original supporting character can carry a similar approachable tone.
