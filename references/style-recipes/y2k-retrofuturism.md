# y2k-retrofuturism — Y2K / Aero-Inspired Retrofuturism

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Specialty / Genre. Music, fashion, playful campaigns, and intentional technological nostalgia.
**Reference points**: Y2K graphics, iMac G3 advertising, Aqua interfaces, and later Aero imagery. These are related but distinct references; choose the period and cues that fit the brief.

## Starting palette and type

- Ice blue `#A3D5FF`; silver `#E0E4EA`; chrome highlights from `#C4C9D2` to `#F2F4F8`.
- Cyan `#5FC9F8` and magenta `#FF66C4` can create an iridescent effect; acid green `#A8FF00` can add emphasis.
- Use black or white text according to the actual background contrast.
- Eurostile or Sharp Grotesk can support a technological display voice; Frutiger or Avenir can support a more humanist reading layer.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24; compact groups can coexist with larger focal objects.
- Translucent surfaces, inner highlights, and radii such as 16 / 24 / 32px can evoke glass or jelly-like controls.
- Chrome lettering, specular highlights, dimensional shapes, and iridescent image treatment can create a clear period reference.
- Use motion such as a gentle reflection sweep or buoyant transition where it supports the experience, with a reduced-motion alternative.
- Keep functional text and controls distinct from decorative effects.

## Tradeoffs

A nostalgic layer can be concentrated in a hero or selected controls while longer reading stays calm. Sharp geometry, muted areas, and restrained type can provide useful contrast. Choose historical coherence deliberately instead of combining every effect associated with early digital design.
