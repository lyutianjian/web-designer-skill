# raycast — Raycast-Inspired Productivity

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Modern Tool / Builder SaaS. Launchers, productivity tools, and interfaces where shortcuts and focused actions are meaningful.
**Reference points**: Raycast application captures, product pages, and extension listings.

## Starting palette and type

- Charcoal `#0F0F11`; translucent surface `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`; border `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)`.
- Text `#FFFFFF` and `#B0B3B8`; red `#FF6363` as a possible identity or action accent.
- Small extension or category accents can use lime, coral, lavender, or cyan when their meaning is clear.
- Display options: Söhne or Inter Tight around weight 600; body Inter or the project family; SF Mono or JetBrains Mono for shortcuts.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64; radius examples 8 / 12 / 16 / 24px by role.
- A focused command surface can float above a quieter backdrop. A shadow such as `0 16px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` can establish separation where needed.
- Translucency and color can introduce depth while preserving the readability of rows, icons, and selection states.
- Shortcut chips should describe working commands. Category colors should remain distinguishable without relying on hue alone.
- Mild spring motion can emphasize a key transition; quick, restrained feedback often suits repeated actions.

## Tradeoffs

A command-palette hero is useful when it explains the actual product. Other interfaces can borrow the layering and focus without inventing a launcher. Button style, accent density, and motion should fit the audience and existing interaction system.
