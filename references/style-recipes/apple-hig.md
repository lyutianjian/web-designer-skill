# apple-hig — Apple-Inspired Product Presentation

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Editorial / Minimalist. Product storytelling with generous space, precise type, and a clear visual focal point.
**Reference points**: Apple product pages and keynote compositions. Consult current platform guidance separately for platform-specific behavior.

## Starting palette and type

- Ground: white `#FFFFFF` or black `#000000`, according to imagery and contrast.
- Ink `#1D1D1F`; soft surface `#F5F5F7`; muted text `#86868B`; accent blue `#0071E3`.
- SF Pro can be appropriate where available and permitted; use the project's platform stack or a suitable alternative such as Inter Tight otherwise.
- Body text around 17px and line height around 1.5 are starting points. Check muted captions against their actual background.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 160; let the product and viewing distance determine section spacing.
- Moderate radii, for example 12 / 18 / 22px, quiet elevation, and controlled ease-out motion can support a precise feel.
- Give a product image or real interface capture enough room to explain itself. Pair it with a strong headline and a clear next action.
- Use thin dividers and typographic emphasis for specifications where helpful. Vary sections with the content rather than repeating one hero formula.

## Tradeoffs

The same approach can support hardware or software when the focal asset is meaningful. Cards, tabs, multiple actions, gradients, or darker scenes can be appropriate; preserve their function and consistency. Generous space should not conceal comparison data or essential controls.

## Optional image study

> Single product on white #FFFFFF ground, diffused studio light, subtle floor reflection, neutral color grading, generous negative space, 16:9. Use real supplied assets for identifiable products.
