# balenciaga-post-2017 — Balenciaga-Inspired Post-2017 Web Minimalism

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Brutalist / Raw. Fashion, cultural campaigns, and deliberately stark product presentation.
**Reference points**: Archived fashion web and campaign material from the period the user intends.

## Starting palette and type

- Black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, and off-white `#F5F4F0` form a stark starting palette.
- Helvetica or Arial can establish a direct, familiar typographic voice; use the project's requirements to decide substitutes.
- Large uppercase headlines can contrast with restrained metadata. Scale the contrast to actual copy and viewport dimensions.
- Additional color can come from photography, identity, or functional state when the composition benefits.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 8 / 16 / 24 / 40; a compact, direct rhythm can support the raw character.
- Crisp edges and flat surfaces can frame editorial imagery without elaborate interface decoration.
- Strong left alignment, tight display tracking, and deliberate crops can create tension.
- Plain text navigation and concise captions work when their actions and meaning remain clear.
- Direct-flash photography and unconventional poses can be reference cues for an appropriate campaign.

## Tradeoffs

Fashion references do not justify hiding actions, shrinking essential text, or removing focus and hover feedback. Buttons should remain recognizable when that helps use. Adapt the degree of austerity to the audience and conversion needs; the palette and type do not need to become universal bans.
