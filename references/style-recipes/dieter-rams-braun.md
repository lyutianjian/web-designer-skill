# dieter-rams-braun — Dieter Rams / Braun-Inspired Utility

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Editorial / Minimalist. Functional clarity for hardware, audio, industrial tools, and specification-led interfaces.
**Reference points**: Braun T3, ET66, and Rams's design writing.

## Starting palette and type

- Ground `#E4E1DC` or `#F5F4F0`; ink `#191919`; grays `#A8A8A8` and `#5C5C5C`.
- Orange `#E96A26` or yellow `#F5C518` can highlight meaningful controls or states.
- Grotesque options: Akzidenz-Grotesk, Helvetica Now, or Söhne. Regular body and medium emphasis can establish restraint; increase weight when clarity requires it.
- Use tabular figures where aligned measurements or specifications aid comparison.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 64 / 128, driven by grouping and use.
- Crisp edges or modest 2–4px radii, quiet surfaces, and brief state feedback can make the interface feel like equipment.
- Present useful specifications directly and label controls in plain language.
- Product diagrams and measurement callouts can clarify construction. Measurements should be real or explicitly marked as illustrative.
- Use accent signals consistently; accompany color with a label or another meaningful cue.

## Tradeoffs

Functional restraint can coexist with warmth, curves, imagery, and stronger emphasis. Avoid hiding essential states or reducing readability to maintain an austere look. An emotional campaign may need additional narrative or imagery beyond a specification-led composition.
