# are-na — Are.na-Inspired Content First

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Brutalist / Raw. Research collections, creative communities, and archives with restrained interface decoration.
**Reference points**: Are.na collections, publication layouts, and deliberately simple web interfaces.

## Starting palette and type

- White `#FFFFFF`; black `#000000`; border gray `#CCCCCC`; optional warm surface `#F4F1E8`.
- Browser-like link colors `#0000EE` and `#551A8B` can make navigation familiar.
- System serif or sans families, including Times or Georgia where appropriate, can support the plain-web character.
- Use a readable text size and line length; the appearance of simplicity should not require tiny copy.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32, adjusted to distinguish related items and separate reading groups.
- Flat rectangular surfaces, fine borders, and visible links can keep attention on the collection itself.
- Let lists, tables, prose, and images retain their natural structures when that helps understanding.
- Headings can use modest changes in size or weight rather than a separate display treatment.
- Quiet transitions and content-rich navigation can support browsing without turning it into a spectacle.

## Tradeoffs

A raw presentation can still be polished in alignment, accessibility, and interaction. Modals, tooltips, custom fonts, or motion may be useful when they solve a real problem. Preserve clear focus, selection, and loading feedback. Borrow the content-first approach without deliberately making the interface difficult or unfinished.

## Optional image study

> Editorial collage on plain white paper, varied image sources with clear spacing and a lightly handmade arrangement, 4:3. Use actual collection material when the images represent real entries.
