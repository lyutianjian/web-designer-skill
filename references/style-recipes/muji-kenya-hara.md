# muji-kenya-hara — MUJI / Kenya Hara-Inspired Restraint

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Editorial / Minimalist. Quiet everyday objects, materials, and understated product stories.
**Reference points**: MUJI packaging and retail environments, Kenya Hara's book White.

## Starting palette and type

- Warm ground `#F4F2EC`; ink `#2A2A28`; secondary text `#7C7B76`; divider `#D9D6CD`.
- Red `#C8161D` can provide a restrained identity or action cue when appropriate.
- Humanist sans options: Söhne, Inter Tight, or Calibre; start with regular text and subtle tracking.
- Noto Sans CJK JP can support Japanese text. Body sizes around 15–16px and generous line spacing are examples to adjust for legibility.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 8 / 16 / 32 / 48 / 96 / 160; generous gaps make sense when the content can sustain them.
- Square or gently softened edges, quiet surfaces, and subtle fades can preserve a calm rhythm.
- Let an everyday object sit within ample space, with a plain caption and clear material detail.
- Use restrained labels and fine rules where they structure information. Hierarchy can still be strong without decorative display effects.

## Tradeoffs

The desired quietness does not set a whitespace percentage, color ban, or restriction on navigation patterns. Keep comparison data, prices, tabs, and important actions visible when the task needs them. Adapt density and contrast for fast scanning or small screens.
