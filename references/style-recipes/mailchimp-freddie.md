# mailchimp-freddie — Mailchimp-Inspired Illustrated Warmth

Illustrative cues, not an official brand specification. Supplied references and accessibility needs take precedence.

**Context**: Warm Humanist. Small-business tools, creator products, education, and approachable services.
**Reference points**: Mailchimp's Freddie-era illustration and identity material, especially the period requested by the user.

## Starting palette and type

- Yellow `#FFE01B`; white `#FFFFFF`; ink `#241C15`; cream `#FBEFE3`.
- Pink `#FF4D74` or a warm coral can add emphasis when it fits the composition.
- Friendly grotesque options: Helvetica Now Display, Söhne, or Inter Tight, with expressive headings and comfortable reading text.
- Body text around 17–18px and line height around 1.6 are starting points. Occasional handwritten details can add warmth when legible.

## Composition cues

- Spacing example: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64; radius examples 8 / 16 / 24px.
- Warm surfaces, confident outlines, and gently irregular illustration can give the page personality.
- Yellow sections can emphasize meaningful moments in the story rather than serving as repetitive filler.
- Direct, conversational copy and useful examples can make a complex product approachable.
- Slightly tilted illustration or buoyant motion can add energy while the underlying reading order stays stable.

## Tradeoffs

Keep illustrations coherent in line, texture, and character. Use authorized assets for the actual Freddie mascot; original supporting illustration can be sourced or generated when appropriate. A specific commissioning method, color count, or alignment is not required. Adapt warmth and motion to the audience rather than excluding enterprise use categorically.
